Title: Why AI Agents Ignore Your Design System: Nokia’s Fix - Joel D'Silva

Joel D'Silva walks through the four systems Nokia's design team built to keep coding agents inside an enterprise design system.

Main Takeaways

- The context was always in the repo. What agents needed was blinders, not more context.
- Design MD files drift. Agents keep reaching for public patterns your enterprise can't ship.
- Validation scripts feed violations back into the agent loop. No raw hex survives a session.
- Log where the agent got stuck to searchable JSON, not markdown. Deterministic beats prose.
- His Figma plugin cuts a screen's context from 17,000 tokens to roughly 1,400.
