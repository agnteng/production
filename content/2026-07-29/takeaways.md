# Main takeaways

What someone would say they learned at this meetup, from its talks and Q&A.
One per line: `- **Claim.** One or two sentences.`

- **AI turned makers into reviewers.** People no longer do three or four tasks a day; they review twenty. The constant small wins wear down attention in a way you don't feel while working.
- **Burnout arrives off the clock.** It shows up on a Sunday evening, not during the work, and replacing a burned-out teammate costs about six months of salary. Teams sandbox their agents carefully and set no limits for themselves.
- **Claude Code can run your life admin.** On a fixed $100 a month, one personal agent built on Claude Code handled an insurance claim, a homeowners' association dispute and a business buyout in a single week.
- **People learn new environments by testing hypotheses.** Given a puzzle current AI can't solve and no instructions, people solved it by proposing a rule, testing it and revising it on a partial picture of the world.
- **That's what today's AI is missing.** Neural networks transfer skills well but adapt badly to unfamiliar settings. Flexible AI needs hypothesis testing built in.
