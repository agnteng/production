Title: AI Agent Sent 52 Million Crypto Tokens by Mistake - Jean-Loïc Mugnier

Jean-Loïc Mugnier walks through real incidents of AI agents losing funds on-chain and lays out a framework for securing agentic systems that touch crypto.

Main Takeaways

- With agents holding crypto, "any kind of mistake is a financial loss".
- Lobstar Wilde misread the digits and sent 52 million tokens, worth about 500,000 then.
- aixbt lost 55 ETH through a vulnerable control panel, not through the model.
- Their Ethereum Foundation research: a safe-looking simulation can still drain your whole wallet.
- Zero trust: constrain what agents can do with policies and enforcement contracts that revert transactions.
