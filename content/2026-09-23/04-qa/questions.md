#### What's the actual attack vector: an exploited device, or an inference provider being subpoenaed?

**Jeremy Healsmith:** The case he has in mind is aid workers in Gaza whose
machines get subpoenaed or simply requisitioned by the IDF. From the session
logs alone you can identify exactly who those workers have been talking to. So
the main threat is a government agency, but a sophisticated attacker could
also sit in the middle of the traffic.
