Title: My Content Team Is a Group Chat of Grok Bots - Tim Haldorsson

Tim Haldorsson shows the Grok Bot setup behind his marketing agency: one bot per part of his day, then group chats where several work as a team.

Main Takeaways

- His Intel bot replaced five or six daily news sites with two briefings a day.
- An "overheard" bot watches Reddit and X for brand mentions the team used to miss.
- From a CRM of around 1,000 contacts, a bot finds similar accounts and drafts openers.
- His content "team" is several agents in one chat, each with its own history.
- Grok Bot is free with X Premium for now, with X API credits built in.
