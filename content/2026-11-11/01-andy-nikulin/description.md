### [Cheap Tokens: A Field Guide to Discounted Inference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6b9P4dJNllw)

Andy Nikulin walks through the grey market for discounted AI tokens, from subscription arbitrage to token "transfer stations", and what the discount costs you.

**Main Takeaways**

*   SemiAnalysis got over $11,000 of API-priced tokens from a $200 Anthropic subscription
*   Brokers resell corporate discounts, farmed credits, idle subscriptions and stolen accounts
*   One station sold Gemini 4 Carbon at 55% off within days of launch
*   The catch: swapped models, pricier cached tokens, harvested prompts, deposits that vanish
*   He only uses them for apps that are "not super important and not super secret"
