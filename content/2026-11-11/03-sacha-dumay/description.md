### [Your SaaS Is Becoming an Agent](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XX3vqa-PjTk)

Sacha Dumay explains why Cashflowy moved its chat from a side panel to the centre of the product, and rebuilt its AI bookkeeper on Vercel's open-source eve.

**Main Takeaways**

*   Users who asked the side-panel chat questions were almost twice as likely to pay
*   His weekend TanStack chat lived in the browser and couldn't run long tasks
*   In eve, user approvals run in code, because an LLM can't be trusted to confirm
*   "Workflow is so outdated." One central agent with 47 tools replaced them
*   Evals run in CI, like the one keeping users from overwriting the ledger
