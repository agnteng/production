# Title practice: sources and strength of evidence

Researched 2026-11-14. YouTube's own guidance on titles is short; almost
everything else published is opinion from tool vendors.

## Official (YouTube)

| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| "Be accurate": a title that misrepresents the video makes viewers stop watching, which hurts discoverability. | [Thumbnail & title tips](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/12340300) |
| "Be succinct" and put the most important words near the beginning; viewers may only see part of the title. | same |
| Two kinds work: searchable titles that outline what to expect, and intriguing titles that spark curiosity. Choose by who will watch and where they find it. | same |
| Limit ALL CAPS and emoji. Save episode numbers and branding for the end. | same |
| Title and thumbnail are seen together and should "create interest and tell a story" together. | same |
| Up to three titles can be A/B tested per video; the winner is chosen on watch time, not clicks; titles that are too similar make the test run longer. | [A/B test titles & thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13861714) |
| A title is at most 100 characters and may not contain `<` or `>`. | YouTube Data API, `videos` resource |

## Practitioner guidance (opinion; sources disagree on the numbers)

- Roughly 60 to 70 characters show in search and fewer on mobile; advice
  clusters on 50 to 60 characters. We use 55 before the speaker's name and
  treat the first 40 as what must stand alone.
- Put the main keyword early. One 2026 vendor study reports that a keyword in
  the first 40% of the title helps ranking; its method is not published.
- An unresolved tension the video resolves earns clicks; vendor claim.

Found in: [AIR Media Tech](https://air.io/en/audience-growth/how-to-write-a-youtube-title-that-gets-clicked-research-across-11-niches),
[channelpreview](https://channelpreview.vercel.app/blog/youtube-title-length),
[Influencer Marketing Hub](https://influencermarketinghub.com/youtube-character-limits/).

## Not found

- No official truncation length for any surface.
- Nothing on whether a title and thumbnail should complement or repeat each
  other beyond "tell a story together"; the complement rule comes from the
  thumbnail guides in `../../thumbnail-critic/references/best-practices.md`.
- Nothing specific to talks or conference recordings. "Title - Speaker" is
  this channel's convention, and it matches YouTube's "branding at the end".
